The Old Bookstore

Page 1

Rain poured down over the quiet downtown street. Lila had lost her way while searching for an apartment she planned to rent. Down a narrow alley, she spotted an old bookstore with dim warm light leaking from its dusty windows. There was no sign outside, only a worn wooden door slightly open. Curiosity pulled her inside. The air smelled of aged paper and damp wood. Shelves stretched high toward the ceiling, filled with countless old books. The shop owner was nowhere to be seen. On a central table lay one locked black notebook. Before she could touch it, a soft voice echoed from the darkness…
